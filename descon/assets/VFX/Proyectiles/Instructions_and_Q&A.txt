###  Common Questions  ###

• Do these effects work in Compatibility mode or Forward+? 
Yes,kind of.. they work in both modes. In Compatibility mode, the effects may appear slightly brighter, you can easily adjust the brightness if needed. Problem is Apple/Mac devices, sometimes things are off.

• Does this work in any version of Godot? 
Not exactly. Most of my effects are designed for Godot 4.3 and above. If you're using an older version, I can't guarantee full compatibility.

• Can I move the "VFX" folder out of the root directory? 
Yes, once it's placed in the main directory, you can move it elsewhere. Just note that demo scenes might break, but the VFX assets themselves should still work fine.

• Will updating Godot break the effects? 
No, unless the Godot team makes major changes to the internal shader system. As long as they keep things stable, the effects should continue to work.
